# Project instructions

- Read README.md and the relevant files in docs/ before implementation.
- Build a complete, self-service Chatbase competitor. Do not restart generic startup-idea research or reject features because competitors offer them.
- Preserve the initial focus: website chat, customer-facing knowledge, email handoff, comparison, and a versioned correction workflow.
- Follow the must-have, optional, and excluded scope in docs/mvp-and-workflows.md. Expanding scope requires an explicit product decision.
- Treat prices, performance targets, and model costs as documented assumptions until measured. Never claim superior accuracy from an unreviewed model-judge score.
- Keep tenant isolation, source provenance, deletion behavior, idempotent metering, and bounded AI spending integral to implementation.
- Maintain the relevant canonical doc when a product or architectural decision changes. Distinguish planned, implemented, and verified behavior.
- Do not claim the public name or domain is cleared. Lattice is currently a working label.
